## 4.8.1-rc2 — Round Ends on Time

A handful of fixes for the next party night. Most of them are about the TV and the host's phone showing what is actually happening.

### 🎯 Smoother rounds

- A steal by the last player ends the round straight away again. Thanks to **@triluch** for reporting it.
- In an intro round the TV timer waits for the host instead of running down while nobody hears a song.
- Encore and the finale tiebreaker now work in games that go from easy to hard songs.
- Players knocked out in Sudden Death no longer turn up as "Closest · Exact!" on the reveal.

### 📺 TV and host phone

- When the game ends, the TV leaves the podium and shows "No active game".
- The light buttons in the host drawer on your phone work again.
- The TV keeps the sabotage and finalist badges when the answer is revealed.
- A My Music game from one of your Music Assistant playlists plays every song setup counted as usable, and broken music links in 40s & 50s Classics and 70s Hits are fixed.
