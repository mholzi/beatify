## 4.4.2-rc1 — Nothing Stuck

Fixes for the moments where a game stopped moving and nobody in the room could tell why.

### ⏱️ Rounds that keep moving

A speaker hiccup between rounds used to freeze every screen on the reveal until someone reloaded. Fixed — along with rounds that ended before the song played, announcements eating into the guessing time, and Sudden Death rounds running the full timer after everyone left had already answered.

### 📱 Your phone is a proper remote

Resume or end a paused game straight from your phone. A start that fails now says why instead of sitting on "Starting…", and the volume buttons show the real level.

### 🙏 Thank you

Everyone who keeps reporting the odd moments. Most of this release came from chasing them.
