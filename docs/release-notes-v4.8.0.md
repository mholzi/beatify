## 4.8.0 — 4.5

Tonight's game plays exactly the songs you picked, and the TV looks the part from the first guest to the final podium.

### 🎛️ Your songs, your setup

Crate Digger is now called My Music, and setup can point it at one of your Music Assistant playlists, showing how many songs are usable, like "37 of 45". The ready card speaks your language and uses the labels you just tapped, and the host page fits small phones. Playlist search puts the best match first, and broken links in four playlists play again.

### 📺 Everyone in the picture

Guests appear as big name tiles on the lobby wall, and the podium takes centre stage at game over. A phone that reloads on the podium follows the host into the rematch. A guest you sit out stays on your leaderboard with "Bring back", and a game with all songs just says "Round 7".
