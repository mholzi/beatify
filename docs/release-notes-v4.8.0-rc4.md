## 4.8.0-rc4 — 4.5

Tonight's game plays exactly the songs you picked, and the TV looks the part from the first guest to the final podium.

### 🎛️ Your songs, your setup

Setup can point My Music at one of your Music Assistant playlists and shows how many songs are usable, like "37 of 45". The ready card speaks your language and uses the labels you just tapped, and the host page fits small phones. Playlist search puts the best match first, and broken links in Trance Classics, Finnish Schlager Classics and EDM Anthems play again.

### 📺 Everyone in the picture

Guests appear as big name tiles on the lobby wall, and the podium takes centre stage at game over. A guest you sit out stays on your leaderboard with "Bring back", and their phone says "no guess" instead of "Exact!". A game with all songs just says "Round 7", even after the TV reloads, and the Sudden Death and encore screens look right on phones again.
