## 4.2.1-rc1 — As Long As You Want It

A party should end when you want it to, not when the playlist runs out. This build lets the host decide how many rounds a game runs — and it fixes a handful of screens that were quietly showing up without their good clothes on.

### 🎮 Choose how long the game runs

Step 4 of the setup now offers **10, 20, 30, all songs, or a number you type in**. Pick 20 and you get a game that fits an evening; pick "all" and nothing changes from before. The estimate under the buttons tells you roughly how long that will take, and a rematch keeps the length you chose. Thanks to **@Vanman777**, who asked for this back in June.

### ✨ Screens that finally look the way they were built

A few parts of the game were styled in the source but never made it to your screen — the "you're out" view in Sudden Death, the shield that saves a streak, and the continue button in the Mix tab. They all look the way they were meant to now.

### 🏆 Sudden Death says what actually happened

When the rounds run out with more than one player still alive, nobody is the last one standing — the win goes to the best of the survivors, and the final screen now says so, along with how many were still in it. When one player really does outlast everyone, the trophy still comes out.

### 🎉 A Wiesn playlist for the Oktoberfest season

**Wiesn Party Hits** joins the catalog with 100 tracks, from the tent classics everyone shouts along to the newer ones that took over the benches in the last few years.

### 🙏 Thank you

To **@Vanman777** for the round-count request and the patience while it found its way into the setup screen. And to everyone who keeps reporting the small things — most of what changed here started as someone saying "that looks odd".
