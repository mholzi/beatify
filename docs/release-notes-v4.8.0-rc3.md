## 4.8.0-rc3 — 4.5

Tonight's game can play exactly the songs you picked, and the TV looks the part from the first guest to the final podium.

### 🎛️ My Music plays from your own playlist

Setup can now point My Music at one of your Music Assistant playlists instead of your whole library. It tells you how many songs are usable, like "37 of 45", and how to bring back the rest. A phone that reloads on the podium still makes the rematch.

### 📺 A TV that fills the room

Guests appear as big name tiles on the lobby wall the moment they join, and the podium takes centre stage at game over. A game with all songs just says "Round 7", on screen and in the spoken welcome. The year and the join card fit on 720p TVs, and the Sudden Death, encore and ghost league screens look right on phones again.

### 🧹 Underneath

Playlist search puts the best match first, and broken links in Finnish Schlager Classics and EDM Anthems play again.
