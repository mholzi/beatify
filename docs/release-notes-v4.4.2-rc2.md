## 4.4.2-rc2 — Nothing Unsaid

Fourteen fixes for moments the game handled correctly and then kept to itself.

### 📺 The room can see what happened

A sabotage now shows up in the row of the player it hit, so the person who froze someone's timer gets the credit they were after. Two players in a finale playoff are marked as finalists instead of the six others wearing skulls. And when your shield absorbs a wrong answer, the reveal finally says so — the streak survived, and now you know why.

### 🎛️ Settings that stay set

Sudden Death and your round count no longer vanish when you tap a chip after the wizard. The tiebreaker fires even if nobody presses Next, a paused game picks the reveal back up on its own, and stopping a song keeps it stopped.

### 🌍 In your language

Amazon Music setup, the submit flow and the TV podium were English for everyone. All six languages now.

### 🙏 Thank you

Every odd moment somebody bothered to write down.
