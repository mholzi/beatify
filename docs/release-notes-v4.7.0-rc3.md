## 4.7.0-rc3 — The Turn

The end of a game now shows the moment it was decided, and the reveal shows where the whole room guessed.

### 📈 The end screen replays the round that decided the game

Three seconds before the podium appears, the TV goes back to the round where the lead changed hands for the last time. Two lines climb, a mark drops on the round, and underneath: *Dana overtakes Ben*. A game somebody led from the first song skips straight to the podium. And when two players finish level, both now stand on the podium with the same place.

### 📍 The reveal puts every guess on a year axis

The TV reveal no longer stops at a top three. Every player's guess sits on a line of years, with initials, stacked where people picked the same year, and on a 1080p TV the whole stack stays on screen. Phones get the same axis, and the year slider's labels no longer run into each other.

### 🙏 Thank you

**@slangreck** reported six TV and slider problems in one afternoon, each with a screenshot. Duplicate leaderboard rows, an unreadable ranking on the reveal, an overflowing fun fact, shrunken award cards and a misplaced status dot are all fixed.
