## 4.8.2 — Does What It Says

A patch for the next party night: when you tap something, Beatify now does what it says.

### 🎯 Rounds that listen

"Next Round" on the host phone takes taps again, thanks to **@nirvdrum** for spotting it. Stop Song stays stopped even when a guest answers right after, a steal locks the guest's screen like a normal answer, and the TV answer counter no longer waits for eliminated players or spectators. In Title & Artist mode the announcement reads out both title and artist, another catch by **@nirvdrum**. Opening the admin page on a second device no longer breaks the first one.

### 🌍 Your language, everywhere

A language change in an open lobby now reaches the TV and the announcements, and error messages on the host phone and the offline page speak all six languages. New for Diwali: **Bollywood Party Hits**, 79 songs. Broken links in 2010s & 2020s Hits and 60s Classics play again.
