## 4.7.3 — Back in the Room

Three fixes for a host who steps away and comes back, a data audit of the anime playlist, and a lot more songs that play on YouTube Music.

### 🚪 The host gets back in after a reload

Reloading the player view, or letting iOS drop the tab, used to leave the host on "Connecting…" with a paused game that could not be resumed. The page now reconnects the way every other player does.

### ⏸️ A pause during the intro start holds

Pausing in the seconds while an intro song was starting could play the song through the whole break, or leave the next round in silence. The intro now stops as soon as it starts, and resumes from the top with the full round time.

### 🎛️ The lobby follows your playlist change

Picking different playlists in setup and going back to the lobby kept the old songs. The game now plays what the screen shows, and everyone who already joined stays in.

### 🎤 Seven anime openings played the wrong recording

Three were name collisions — the band SCANDAL against a song called "Scandal", SID against Sido, a generic "My Hero Academia" cue against a 2025 spin-off track. One was an instrumental Season 3 version dated 2019 instead of 2013. A duplicate entry and three dead Tidal links went with them.

### 📺 More songs on YouTube Music

243 new links across 17 playlists. YouTube reach is 95.8 %, up from 93.0 %.

### 🇿🇦 New: South Africa by Q

89 tracks of Amapiano, Afro House, Afrobeats and Highlife.
