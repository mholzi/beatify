## 4.8.3-rc4 — Wait for It

Intro rounds now tell the whole room what is going on.

### ⚡ Intro rounds, on every screen

- While an intro round waits for the host to start it, the TV shows a big "Intro Round!" card with the rule in one sentence. Before, it looked like a normal round. Thanks to **@aenkieh** for pointing at it.
- When the host skips an intro round instead of starting it, the "Waiting for host…" window on the guests' phones closes with the round.

### 🎧 Playing your own music

Title & Artist games from "My Music" now start even when your library has no verified release years yet. The mode never asks for a year, so it no longer waits for one, and the song count in the settings shows what the game will really draw from. Thanks to **@nirvdrum** for the report.

### 🔇 No stray songs

When Apple Music is slow and Beatify skips a song, that song no longer starts by itself a round later.

### 🎵 Playlist repairs

"Gotcha", the Starsky & Hutch theme, now sits in 1976, the year it was first heard on the show, after a guest flagged it mid-game. "More Than Words" and "Shine" have fresh Apple Music links in the 90s playlist. Seven more broken links are repaired in Hitster 100 Français and Hitster Brasil.
