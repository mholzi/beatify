## 4.8.1-rc1 — Round Ends on Time

Three small fixes for the next party night, all about a round doing what everyone at the table expects.

### 🎯 Smoother rounds

- A steal by the last player ends the round straight away again, instead of waiting for the timer. Thanks to **@triluch** for reporting it.
- A My Music game from one of your Music Assistant playlists plays every song setup counted as usable.
- The TV keeps the sabotage and finalist badges on the leaderboard when the answer is revealed.
