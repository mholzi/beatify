## 4.8.0-rc1 — 4.5

Tonight's game can play exactly the songs you picked for the party, and nobody gets left out of the rematch.

### 🎛️ Crate Digger plays from your own playlist

Setup now has a switch between the whole library and one of your Music Assistant playlists. The screen tells you how many songs are usable, like "37 of 45", and why the rest were left out, each reason with a way to fix it. The playlist is read again when the game starts, so last-minute edits count, and songs from the last game stay in.

### 🏆 A reload on the podium still makes the rematch

A phone that reloads during the final standings reconnects and follows the host into the next round. A guest who scans the QR code then gets the join form as soon as the new lobby opens, and an old QR link leads to the new game.

### 🧹 Underneath

Two dead Tidal links in Divorced Dad Rock play again.
