## 4.8.1 — Round Ends on Time

A patch for the next party night: the TV and your phone now show what is actually happening in the room.

### 🎯 Rounds that end on time

When the last player steals an answer, the round ends right away, thanks to **@triluch** for spotting it. In an intro round the TV timer waits for the host instead of running down in silence. Encore and the finale tiebreaker now work in games that go from easy to hard songs, and players knocked out in Sudden Death no longer show up as "Closest" on the reveal.

### 📺 TV and host phone

When the game ends, the TV leaves the podium straight away. The light buttons in the host drawer on your phone work again, the "End round" card says "1 player" instead of "1 players", and the TV keeps the sabotage and finalist badges at the reveal. My Music plays every song your playlist check counted, and broken links in 40s & 50s Classics and 70s Hits play again.
