## 4.2.1-rc2 — Bring Your Own Records

Beatify has always played from a streaming service. This build lets it play from **your** music instead — the albums you ripped, the files on your drive, the collection you have been curating for years. And when the game is over, your speaker goes back to what it was doing before you started it.

### 🎧 Play from your own library

Pick your Music Assistant library — Plex, Jellyfin, local files — instead of a streaming service, and the game draws its songs from your own collection. Getting this right meant more than pressing play: your files' tags often carry the year of the *pressing*, not the year of the song, so a shelf full of greatest-hits rips would have made half of music history look like the nineties. Beatify checks the facts before it asks you a question about them. Huge thanks to **@DrMagicWolf**, who built this and saw it through.

### 🎵 Your music comes back when the game ends

Beatify takes over the speaker's queue while it plays. Until now it never gave it back: the party ended and your own listening was gone. Now the track you were playing returns when the game does — paused, right where you left it, at the volume you had it at. That last part matters even if you switch rooms mid-game; each speaker is handed back the way you left it.

### 📺 The standings fit the screen

On a television across the room, the scoreboard used to run past the bottom edge and quietly take the last players with it — and there was no way to scroll a TV screen to find them. The rows now share the space they have and the text scales to fit, so everyone stays visible however many of you are playing. Thanks to **@FurtiveD** for reporting it.

### 🙏 Thank you

To **@DrMagicWolf** for the library mode, built almost entirely unprompted, and to **@FurtiveD** for playing every day and telling us when something looks wrong. Most of what changed here started with someone saying "that looks odd" — please keep saying it.
