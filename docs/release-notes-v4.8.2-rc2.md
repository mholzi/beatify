## 4.8.2-rc2 — Does What It Says

A round of fixes for host and guests: when you tap something, it now does what it says.

### 🎯 Rounds that listen

- "Next Round" takes taps again while the host drawer is open. Thanks to **@nirvdrum** for reporting it.
- Stop Song stays stopped, even when a guest answers right after or an announcement is still playing.
- A steal locks the guest's screen like a normal answer.
- The TV answer counter no longer waits for eliminated players or spectators.
- In Title & Artist mode, the announcement reads out both title and artist, also reported by **@nirvdrum**.
- Opening the admin page on a second device no longer breaks the first one. Both keep working.

### 🌍 Your language, everywhere

A language change in an open lobby now reaches the TV and the announcements. Host-phone error messages and the offline page speak all six languages.

### 🎉 Bollywood Party Hits

A new playlist with 79 songs, just in time for Diwali.
