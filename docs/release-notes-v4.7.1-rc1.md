## 4.7.1-rc1 — Clean Endings

Two things that got in the way at the end of a round are gone, and more of the catalogue plays on YouTube Music.

### ⏭️ The End round card closes when the round ends

If the host opened the End round card and the timer ran out first, the card stayed on screen, frozen countdown and all, right through the reveal and even Game Over. It now closes by itself the moment the round is over. **@Rayan-and-beyond** wrote the fix.

### 🏆 The winner's name stays whole on the TV podium

A seven-letter winner like Andreas no longer splits in the middle of the word on the centre stand.

### 🎉 306 more songs on YouTube Music

Nine playlists gained YouTube Music links, and Deutschrap Klassiker and Clásicos del Rock en Español now play in full.

### 🙏 Thank you

**@Rayan-and-beyond** for the End round fix, sent in as a finished pull request.
